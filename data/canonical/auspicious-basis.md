## 1. The scheme: ทักษาเมือง (taksa mueang) — SOLID as documented tradition

Because the city was "born" on a Thursday, the eight taksa qualities lie
clockwise from West: บริวาร (retinue, W) → อายุ (longevity, NW) → เดช (power, N) →
ศรี (glory, NE) → มูละ (wealth, E) → อุตสาหะ (industry, SE) → มนตรี (counsel, S) →
**กาลกิณี (misfortune, SW)**; เกตุ (center) at the city navel.

| Direction | Taksa | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| N | เดช det (power) | Chang Phueak / Hua Wiang Gate |
| NE | ศรี si (glory) | Jaeng Si Phum |
| E | มูละ mula (wealth) | Tha Phae Gate |
| SE | อุตสาหะ utsaha (industry) | Jaeng Katam |
| S | มนตรี montri (minister) | Chiang Mai Gate |
| SW | กาลกิณี kalakini (misfortune) | Jaeng Ku Hueang + Saen Pung Gate sector |
| W | บริวาร boriwan (retinue) | Suan Dok Gate |
| NW | อายุ ayu (longevity) | Jaeng Hua Lin |
| Center | เกตุ ket | สะดือเมือง city navel |

Sources: [Thai Wikipedia ทักษาเวียงเชียงใหม่](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/ทักษาเวียงเชียงใหม่)
[reference], citing **Surapol Damrikul, เสาหลักเมืองและวัดหัวเมืองกับแผนผังเมืองล้านนา, Faculty
of Fine Arts, CMU, 2545/2002 [academic]** and Sanguan Chotisukharat, ตำนานเมืองเหนือ
(2508/1965) [book]; [Chiang Mai News, 17 Mar 2021, citing historian Phudej Saensa](https://www.chiangmainews.co.th/knowledge/1607235/) [news citing academic].

**Caveat (carry in data):** Surapol Damrikul holds the taksa overlay was
formalized *after* the founding era (Tilokarat and later). Scheme = SOLID
documented tradition; its projection back to 1296 = UNVERIFIED reconstruction.
Minor divergence: Chiang Mai News places kalakini at Saen Pung Gate; the
Damrikul/Wikipedia table at Jaeng Ku Hueang — same SW sector, not a real conflict.

## 2. Gate-by-gate

- **Chang Phueak = auspicious royal north entry — SOLID.** Royal/coronation
  entries via Hua Wiang Gate from Mangrai's era; officials entering the province
  traditionally use it "for auspiciousness" (FAD archaeologist Saiklang Jindasu,
  2023 excavation): [Thai PBS 24 Nov 2023](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/334234) [news/official];
  Thai Wikipedia; Chiang Mai Municipality [official]. Pre-entry merit at Wat
  Chiang Yuen — REPORTED: [Matichon Weekly](https://www.matichon.co.th/weekly/column/article_634820) [news].
  Renaming era conflict (Saen Mueang Ma white-elephant monuments vs Kawila
  restoration) — carry both: Thai PBS/Matichon vs Thai Wikipedia/Chang Puak.
- **Saen Pung/Suan Prung = the ghost gate (ประตูผี) — SOLID.** Corpses exit the
  walled city ONLY here, toward the Hai Ya cremation ground; custom persists:
  Thai Wikipedia; Chang Puak Magazine; [Matichon Weekly 17 Jan 2023](https://today.line.me/th/v3/article/rqzORj) [news];
  Top Chiang Mai. Corollary: corpses prohibited via Chiang Mai Gate — REPORTED→SOLID-leaning.
  Executions near the gate — REPORTED (Chang Puak). "Suan Prung = disembowelling"
  etymology — UNVERIFIED/FOLKLORE-COMMON (better-attested: แสนปุง "hundred
  thousand hearths", metalworkers' quarter). Phra Sihing faces the gate as
  suppression — REPORTED (single columnist).
- **Suan Dok (W)**: royal flower garden; **no corpse function found** — any
  "corpses left via the west gate" retelling is UNVERIFIED for Chiang Mai.
- **Tha Phae (E)**: trade/wealth artery — SOLID (function).
- **Chiang Mai Gate (S)**: "tail of city" — SOLID (name); corpse prohibition REPORTED.

## 3. Corners

- **Si Phum (NE)**: most auspicious; wall construction traditionally began here
  — SOLID: Thai Wikipedia; Chang Puak ("most auspicious of the four corners");
  Top Chiang Mai; [CPA Media](https://www.cpamedia.com/article.php?pg=features&aid=200506112800).
- **Ku Hueang (SW)**: kalakini sector — SOLID via taksa; "most ill-fated bastion"
  phrasing REPORTED (Chang Puak). Mün Hueang jailer name-story REPORTED.
- **Hua Lin (NW)**: "head of the water conduit" — moat water enters from Huai
  Kaeo off Doi Suthep — SOLID: [Fine Arts Dept Office 7](https://www.finearts.go.th/fad7/view/46699-แม่ข่า---ลำน้ำ---คู-เมือง----คลอง-และการจัดการน้ำเมืองเชียงใหม่) [official].
- **Katam (SE)**: lowest corner, water outlet, fish-trap etymology — SOLID
  (hydrology+etymology). "Katam = unlucky" — UNVERIFIED (taksa says อุตสาหะ,
  mildly positive).
- **Water rule**: chronicle's ชัยมงคล 7 ประการ includes W→E slope + water from
  Buddha-hallowed Doi Suthep — SOLID: [Thai Wikipedia ชัยมงคล 7 ประการ](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/ชัยมงคล_7_ประการ);
  [Thai PBS interview, Warong Wonglangka, CMU Architecture, 19 Feb 2023](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/324733).
  "NW-in → SE-out auspicious flow" = documented engineering (FAD) + derived
  phrasing — REPORTED as an explicit auspiciousness rule.

## 4. City as body / mandala

Toponymic body-schema (head gate N, water-head NW, navel center, tail gate S,
dead exit SW) — SOLID for toponyms (Thai Wikipedia; [Khaosod](https://www.khaosod.co.th/newspaper/newspaper-inside-pages/news_7295858));
explicit "city as organism" articulation REPORTED (Phudej Saensa via press;
fuller treatment Surapol Damrikul 2545 [academic, print — verify]).
Mandala/axis scholarship: **Tanabe, "Autochthony and the Inthakhin Cult of
Chiang Mai," in Turton (ed.), Civility and Savagery, 2000, 294–318 [academic]**;
**Swearer, Premchit & Dokbuakaew, Sacred Mountains of Northern Thailand, 2004
[academic]**; Denes & Pradit, *JUCR* 25 (2022) ([PDF](https://www.cujucr.com/downloads/Individual%20Articles/25/vol%2025%20Alexandra%20Denes%20&%20Ajirapa%20Pradit.pdf)) [academic].
Founding ritual protected center + five gates — **Wyatt & Aroonrut (trans.),
The Chiang Mai Chronicle, 1998, p. 78** — SOLID [book/academic].

## 5. Sao Inthakhin

Indra's pillar to the Lawa; erected at Wat Sadue Mueang/Wat Inthakhin
("city navel", by Three Kings Monument); **moved to Wat Chedi Luang under
Kawila ca. 1800 (พ.ศ. 2343)**; annual Inthakhin festival (6th northern month,
May–Jun), court-run rites (with mediumship, abolished ca. 1900) became
municipal in the 1960s; medium elements relegated to Jaeng Si Phum
(Tanabe 2000:308). — SOLID: Tanabe 2000; Sanguan 1971; Denes & Pradit 2022;
[en.Wikipedia Inthakhin (pillar)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inthakhin_(pillar)); [Museum Thailand](https://www.museumthailand.com/th/3833/storytelling/วัดอินทขีล/) [official].
Dating controversy (golden-age vs Kawila-era creation): REPORTED —
Pen Supha Sukkhata Jai-in, Matichon Weekly [2011](http://botkwamdee.blogspot.com/2011/08/intakeel25.html), [2024](https://www.matichon.co.th/weekly/column/article_728490).
